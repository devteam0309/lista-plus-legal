Lista+

A customer credit (utang) ledger for small shops · com.jorres.listaplus

Lista+ helps a shop owner keep a record of credit they have already extended to their customers. It works entirely offline, with the ledger stored on the owner's own phone. Cloud backup is optional and only runs after the owner signs in.

Privacy Policy What the app stores, what leaves the phone, and what never does. Account & Data Deletion How to delete a cloud account and its backed-up data, in the app or by email.

Lista+ is a record-keeping tool for tracking credit a shop has already extended. It is not a lender, does not provide credit or financial services, and does not process payments between a shop and its customers.
